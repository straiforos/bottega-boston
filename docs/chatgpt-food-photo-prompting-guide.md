# ChatGPT food photo polish — short guide

Upload the source photo, then paste a prompt built from the blocks below. Ask for a **square 1:1** studio product shot.

---

## Core prompt (dining / single portion)

```
Polish this restaurant food photo of "[Dish name]" into a clean studio product shot.
Menu description: [paste real description].

Keep the same dish identity, ingredients, portion size, and sauce colors—it should still look like what the kitchen serves.

Scene: uniform matte warm-stone countertop (dusty beige limestone, soft grain—no marble, wood, or paper) with a matching seamless backdrop.
Serve on a round matte white ceramic coupe plate (shallow, plain, no logo), portion centered with a little empty plate showing. Don’t leave food bare on the counter or in takeout plastic.
Soft even studio lighting with a gentle contact shadow under the plate. Square 1:1 crop, subject centered.

Don’t paste a cutout onto a new background—relight so the food belongs in the scene (matching perspective, soft shadow, no hard matte edge).
Remove store interior, people, clutter, logos, text, watermarks, and UI. No extra garnish or props.
```

**Drinks:** keep the cup/glass; don’t force a plate under a drink.  
**Coffee:** plain foam/crema only—no latte art.

---

## Extra lines (add only if needed)

| If… | Add… |
|-----|------|
| Cake | One triangular slice on the plate—not the whole cake, not a missing wedge, not a slice leaning on the rest. Keep the exact layer count from the source. |
| Other plated dessert | Must sit on the white coupe plate. |
| Matching another cake’s framing | “Image 1 = this cake. Image 2 = layout only. Match camera, crop, plate size/placement, and slice silhouette from image 2. Keep image 1’s flavor, color, frosting, and layer count—don’t copy image 2’s taste or look.” |

---

## Catering / bulk version

Start from a plated dining photo when you can.

```
Adapt this single-serving studio food photo into a LARGE QUANTITY catering tray shot of "[Dish name]".
Menu description: [description]. [optional note, e.g. “hotel pan of pasta for 12–15”]

Fill the tray generously for a group order (roughly a dozen+ portions when that fits).
Keep the same dish identity, ingredients, sauces, and colors. Don’t invent a different recipe. Don’t keep the single plate.

Same warm-stone scene as above. Vessel = metal hotel pan or packed catering tray—never a dinner plate, marble slab, or parchment.
Soft even light, gentle contact shadow under the tray. Square 1:1, tray centered.
Don’t paste a cutout—relight so the tray sits on the stone.
Remove store interior, people, clutter, logos, text, watermarks, UI.
```

**Quick vessel cues:** salad → deep pan/bowl for ~14–16 · baked → full steel hotel pan, scored · sandwiches → pan packed with many halves · bites → dense tray of dozens · dessert box → open white pastry box packed full · coffee → urn/dispenser for ~16, not one cup · pasta → tossed naturally, no CGI swirls/nests.

---

## Do / don’t

**Do:** name the dish, paste the menu text, lock colors/shapes/layers, square crop, warm stone, soft shadow.  
**Don’t:** invent recipes, latte art, whole cakes for dining shots, single plates for catering, cutout-on-new-bg, logos/text, extra props.

**Tip:** If ChatGPT drifts, reply with only the failing rule, e.g. “Keep two sponge layers only” or “Hotel pan, not a plate.”
